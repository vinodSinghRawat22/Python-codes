Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the technology that allows machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, and problem-solving. It uses data, algorithms, and computational models to make decisions, recognize patterns, and even predict outcomes. AI is widely used in everyday life, powering smart assistants, self-driving cars, personalized recommendations, and healthcare innovations. With its ability to analyze vast amounts of information quickly, AI is transforming industries and shaping the future of technology.
