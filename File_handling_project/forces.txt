The Indian Defence Forces, comprising the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force, play a vital role in safeguarding the nation’s sovereignty and territorial integrity. The Army is responsible for land-based operations, maintaining border security, and conducting counter-insurgency missions. The Navy secures India’s maritime boundaries, protects shipping lanes, and ensures naval dominance in regional waters. The Air Force maintains aerial superiority, conducts surveillance, and provides rapid response during conflicts. Together, these forces work under the Ministry of Defence, supported by paramilitary and strategic units, to ensure national security, disaster response, and peacekeeping operations. Their dedication and discipline make them a symbol of national pride and resilience.
