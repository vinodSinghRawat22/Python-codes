Python is a high-level, versatile programming language known for its simplicity and readability. Developed by Guido van Rossum in the late 1980s, it emphasizes clean syntax that allows programmers to express concepts in fewer lines of code compared to many other languages. Python supports multiple programming paradigms, including procedural, object-oriented, and functional programming, making it highly flexible for different types of projects. Its extensive standard library and vibrant ecosystem of third-party packages enable developers to build applications ranging from web development and data analysis to artificial intelligence and scientific computing. Python’s ease of learning, combined with its powerful capabilities, has made it one of the most popular languages in the world for both beginners and experienced developers.
